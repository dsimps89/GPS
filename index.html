How to read the signal

Intensity is estimated demand relative to the available venue capacity. Opportunity combines activity, time and your known acquaintances; it is not a guarantee of meeting anyone.

What would improve accuracy? Actual venue opening hours and approved capacities, historical event attendance, and voluntary aggregated counts. No personal movement tracking is used.

The heatmap uses synthetic venue locations and a simple Monte Carlo model. It does not map real individuals or show real-time population clusters.
